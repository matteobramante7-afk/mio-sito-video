Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

CreatorStudioAI is a personal content management and video scheduling tool.

1. Information We Collect

When you connect your TikTok account via TikTok Login Kit, we only access basic profile information (open_id, avatar, display name) and content posting permissions authorized by you.

2. How We Use Information

Your authentication tokens and data are used exclusively to schedule and publish original video content to your own TikTok profile. We do not sell, share, or distribute user data to third parties.

3. Data Security & Deletion

You can revoke access at any time directly from your TikTok account settings under "Manage app permissions".
